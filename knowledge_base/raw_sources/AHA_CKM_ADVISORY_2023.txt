CARDIOVASCULAR-KIDNEY-METABOLIC (CKM) HEALTH: A PRESIDENTIAL ADVISORY FROM THE AMERICAN HEART ASSOCIATION
Published by: American Heart Association, Ndumele CE, Rangaswami J, Chow SL, et al. Circulation 2023;148:1606-1635.

SECTION 1: THE CKM SYNDROME CONCEPT AND STAGING
Cardiovascular-Kidney-Metabolic (CKM) syndrome reflects systemic pathological interactions among obesity, insulin resistance, type 2 diabetes, hypertension, dyslipidemia, and chronic kidney disease leading to multiorgan cardiovascular risk.
- Stage 0: No CKM risk factors (ideal cardiometabolic health).
- Stage 1: Excess or dysfunctional adiposity (BMI >= 25 kg/m2 or abdominal obesity) and/or impaired glucose tolerance (prediabetes).
- Stage 2: Established metabolic risk factors including clinical hypertension, hypertriglyceridemia (>= 150 mg/dL), low HDL (< 40 mg/dL men, < 50 mg/dL women), type 2 diabetes, or chronic kidney disease.

SECTION 4: MULTI-SYSTEM LIFESTYLE SYNERGY RECOMMENDATIONS
- Cross-Condition Synergy: Interventions that simultaneously target central adiposity, glycemic excursion, and atherogenic dyslipidemia produce compounding risk reduction.
- Nutrition: Adopt an overall heart-healthy dietary pattern (such as Mediterranean or DASH dietary pattern) characterized by vegetables, fruits, whole grains, lean protein, and unsaturated plant oils, while avoiding trans fats and refined carbohydrates.
- Physical Activity: Accumulate at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity per week to improve skeletal muscle insulin sensitivity, lower triglyceride concentrations, and raise protective HDL cholesterol.
- Sleep & Stress: Support circadian recovery and sleep duration (7 to 9 hours per night) to mitigate autonomic stress and evening cortisol surges that impair glycemic regulation.
