2013 AHA/ACC/TOS GUIDELINE FOR THE MANAGEMENT OF OVERWEIGHT AND OBESITY IN ADULTS (LIFESTYLE SECTIONS ONLY)
Published by: AHA/ACC/TOS Task Force on Practice Guidelines. Circulation 2014;129(25 Suppl 2):S102-S138.
RESTRICTION NOTICE: Ingestion is strictly limited to Section 3.2 (Diets for Weight Loss) and Section 3.3 (Lifestyle Intervention). All outdated pharmacological sections are excluded.

SECTION 3.2: DIETS FOR WEIGHT LOSS
- Energy Deficit Prescription: An energy deficit of 500 to 750 kcal/day is recommended to achieve a clinically meaningful and safe rate of weight loss (typically 1 to 2 lbs per week).
- Caloric Intake Targets: Diets providing 1,200 to 1,500 kcal/day for women and 1,500 to 1,800 kcal/day for men are effective for weight reduction when adjusted for baseline body weight.
- Dietary Composition: Multiple dietary patterns that restrict energy intake and create an energy deficit produce equivalent weight loss; individual preferences and cultural factors should guide dietary selection.

SECTION 3.3: LIFESTYLE INTERVENTION AND WEIGHT LOSS TARGETS
- Initial Weight Loss Goal: Prescribe an initial weight loss goal of 5% to 10% of baseline body weight over a 6-month period.
- Clinical Benefits: Achieving a 5% to 10% weight loss produces significant improvements in blood pressure, triglyceride levels, HDL cholesterol, and fasting blood glucose, substantially lowering cardiovascular risk.
