NATIONAL DIABETES PREVENTION PROGRAM (NATIONAL DPP) PREVENTT2 CURRICULUM (2024 UPDATE)
Published by: Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC), US Department of Health and Human Services.

MODULE 1: WELCOME TO PREVENTT2 AND PROGRAM CORE GOALS
The National Diabetes Prevention Program (National DPP) is an evidence-based lifestyle change program designed to help adults with prediabetes or at high risk for type 2 diabetes prevent or delay the onset of the disease.
Core Lifestyle Goals:
Goal 1: Weight Reduction. Participants work to lose at least 5% to 7% of their starting body weight. Clinical evidence from the DPP trial demonstrates that achieving a 5% to 7% weight reduction reduces the risk of developing type 2 diabetes by 58% in adults (and by 71% in adults aged 60 and older).
Goal 2: Physical Activity. Participants work up to at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity physical activity per week. This breaks down to about 30 minutes a day, 5 days a week. Moderate-intensity activity is movement that makes you breathe faster and your heart beat faster, while still being able to carry on a conversation (such as brisk walking at 3 to 4 miles per hour).

MODULE 2: GET ACTIVE AND TRACKING MOVEMENT
Physical activity helps muscles absorb glucose from the blood for energy, lowering blood sugar levels independently of insulin.
Key Actionable Guidance:
- Start slowly and build up gradually. If you are not currently active, start with 10-minute sessions of brisk walking.
- Accumulate at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity activity across the week.
- Spread activity over at least 3 to 5 days during the week rather than doing all activity on a single day.
- Track minutes of activity daily to monitor weekly progress toward the 150-minute goal.
- Limit sedentary sitting time by standing up or taking light movement breaks every 30 to 60 minutes during prolonged desk or screen time.

MODULE 3: EAT WELL TO PREVENT T2 AND REDUCE CALORIES
Healthy eating focuses on long-term sustainable dietary changes rather than extreme restrictive diets.
Key Actionable Guidance:
- Reduce daily caloric intake by 500 to 750 calories per day to achieve a steady, safe weight loss of 1 to 2 pounds per week.
- Reduce portion sizes of energy-dense foods, especially foods high in saturated fat and added sugars.
- Fill half your plate with non-starchy vegetables (such as spinach, broccoli, peppers, salad greens).
- Fill one quarter of your plate with lean protein (poultry, fish, beans, tofu, eggs).
- Fill one quarter of your plate with whole grains or complex high-fiber carbohydrates (brown rice, oats, quinoa, whole wheat).
- Replace sugar-sweetened beverages (sodas, sweet teas, energy drinks, fruit juices) with water, sparkling water, or unsweetened tea.

MODULE 4: SELF-MONITORING AND SUSTAINED BEHAVIOR CHANGE
Self-monitoring is a critical predictor of successful weight loss and glycemic control.
Key Actionable Guidance:
- Weigh yourself at least once a week at the same time of day on the same scale, recording the weight in your tracking log.
- Track daily food intake and daily physical activity minutes to recognize personal triggers and maintain behavioral accountability.
- Establish realistic, progressive weekly SMART goals (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).
